# Reviewer Response: Dataset Description and Reproducibility

We agree that the original Section 3.2 description was too terse for a replication-oriented paper. In revision, we have replaced the generic wording with an explicit provenance statement and exact monitoring-window description. The revised text now identifies the raw source workbook as `data_table.xlsx`, states that the analysis-ready dataset is deterministically generated by `scripts/ingest_operational_workbook.py`, and reports the exact retained study window as 2024-01-01 00:00:00 through 2024-04-01 07:00:00, comprising 2069 valid hourly observations from the JAN, FEB, MAR, and APR sheets. We also now enumerate the operational channels and units available in the source workbook, including aggregate incomer current, busbar voltages, total MW, and feeder-current channels associated with named 33 kV feeders.

We also agree that the prior phrase "approximately one year" was inaccurate for the dataset actually used in the final analysis. That wording has been removed. The revised manuscript and appendix now report the exact date range, the deterministic raw-to-analysis column mapping, the row inclusion and exclusion rules, and the provenance file generated during ingest. These additions are supported by `METHODS_DATA_APPENDIX.md`, `DATA_PROVENANCE.md`, `FINAL_RUN_MANIFEST.yaml`, and `manuscript/artifacts/research/data_provenance.yaml`.

At the same time, we do not want to overclaim what the released source materials support. The raw workbook does not itself provide a complete utility metadata package covering SCADA vendor, instrument calibration certificates, capacitor-bank inventory, transformer nameplate ratings, feeder impedances, or a utility data-sharing reference number. We have therefore revised the manuscript to state this explicitly as a limitation: the study is reproducible as a computational workflow from the retained field workbook and scripts, but site-level engineering replication beyond those released materials would require additional utility asset and protection records not contained in the workbook.

In short, the revision addresses the concern in two ways:

1. It upgrades the manuscript from a generic dataset description to an exact provenance- and channel-based account of the data actually analysed.
2. It explicitly narrows the reproducibility claim from full external site replication to reproducible computational analysis on a named field-derived dataset, while identifying the missing utility metadata as a limitation rather than leaving it implicit.
