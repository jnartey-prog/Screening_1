ADDITIONAL RESULT STATEMENTS FOR DISCUSSION

1. The retained dataset comprises 2082 hourly observations covering approximately three months, not one year; therefore, any generalization claims should remain bounded to this observation window.
2. The final risk classes are nearly balanced by design, with high-risk states representing 34.01% of the retained observations and low/moderate states each representing 33.00%.
3. High-risk periods are concentrated in evening demand intervals, with the largest hourly high-risk shares at 18:00 (81.61%), 20:00 (75.58%), 19:00 (75.00%), 21:00 (67.86%), and 22:00 (52.33%). This suggests a strong association between peak-demand operation and elevated screening scores.
4. The cleaned provenance confirms that one extreme busbar-voltage outlier was screened before analysis, leading to a final retained `v_bus` range of 32.4-35.9 kV. This should be stated explicitly to demonstrate data-quality control.
5. The artefacts support an operational screening narrative, but not direct validation of harmonic resonance events. In a Q1 manuscript, the discussion should consistently use terms such as screening, susceptibility, prioritization, and watchlist, while avoiding claims of confirmed resonance detection without external harmonic measurements.
